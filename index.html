This test is proctored. Your camera checks that your face stays visible, and a screenshot of your entire screen is captured every 10 seconds and saved with your name, ID, the test and the time. Only an administrator can view these. Monitoring stops as soon as you submit. Your browser will ask for permission; nothing is accessed without it.

⚠ Important rules – read before you start

  1. You must answer using your voice (microphone), and every answer must contain at least 50 words.
  2. You may edit your answer after recording to correct any mistakes before moving on.
  3. Do not try to break or bypass any of the rules above. Proctoring is monitoring you through your camera and screen, and every violation is recorded and reported to the administrator.

Admin workspace.

Prepare the test, then review the answers submitted by Scholar.

Set how long each scholar gets, for example 10, 20 or 30 minutes (or 1 hour). Use 0 for no time limit. The timer starts when the scholar presses Start test and the test is submitted automatically when time ends.

Each question needs an expected answer and at least one keyword. Separate keywords with commas. Capital letters, plurals, word endings (test, tests, testing, tested) and small typos in longer words are matched automatically. For different words with the same meaning, use | inside one keyword, such as CPU | processor | central processing unit.
